# Travel Weather Planner

## Project Overview

The Travel Weather Planner is a decision-making tool that determines whether commuting is feasible based on weather conditions, travel distance, and available transportation options. It uses conditional logic to evaluate real-world commuting scenarios and provide a simple True/False recommendation.

## Objective

Assess commuting viability by analyzing:
- Distance to destination
- Current weather conditions (rain)
- Available transportation (bike, car, ride-share app)
